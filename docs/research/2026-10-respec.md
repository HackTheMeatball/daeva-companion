# Respec: stigma reset and Daevanion reset (0.9 P20, Jane's D1 / D2)

Game Data Check, 2026-10-20. Research only. Read 2026-10-20 with our User-Agent, ≥ 10 s apart: Metabot
`/guides/stigma-guide`, daevaguides `/en/guides/stigmas-guide/` and `/en/guides/daevanion-guide/` (both Full). Facts only.

## D1: stigmas

| Question | Answer | Sources | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does a stigma reset refund the shards? | Yes. Resetting a stigma skill returns the Stigma Shard points spent on it, and the reset messages confirm it | Metabot; daevaguides (credits Metabot) | single source (daevaguides repeats Metabot) |
| Does a reset cost something? | Free at Lv 39 (Global, NA): "no cost to respec even at level 39", and the reset dialog ("Would you like to reset all skills? (Current preset's skills will be reset.)", Cancel / Reset) shows no price. Lv 45 not checked. The research (Metabot; daevaguides) said it costs Kinah; superseded at Lv 39 by the owner's screen | owner, 2026-10-21 (play and screenshot) | owner screen (source of truth); Lv 39 only |
| How much? | Nothing at Lv 39 (row above). KR: 10,000 Kinah per point from 28 Jan 2026, later dropped (daevaguides, from aion2hub / ExpCarry); not shown in the app | owner, 2026-10-21; daevaguides | Global Lv 39: owner screen; other levels unchecked |
| Does unequipping refund or reset? | No. Re-equipping a stigma keeps its levels; only a respec of that skill or of all skills sets it back to Lv 1. A stigma can sit in any slot a skill can (supersedes the earlier "stigma quickslot, Quick Use 9–12" placement from Metabot) | owner, 2026-10-21 | owner-confirmed |
| When can you swap? | Not in combat ("the client blocks swaps in combat") | Metabot | single source (game behaviour as described) |
| Do Daevanion nodes raise stigmas? | No. The boards raise only active and passive skills | Metabot | single source |

## D2: Daevanion

| Question | Answer | Sources | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can you undo a node? | Yes. You remove a node and get its point back | daevaguides | single source |
| One node or a whole board? | Per board: the board window has a reset button next to "Acquire All Nodes" (owner's Vaizel board screenshot). daevaguides describes removing single nodes, and that a node another node depends on can't go | owner, 2026-10-21 (screenshot); daevaguides | per-board reset: owner screen |
| Cost | The owner's screen doesn't show the price of the board reset. KR: 500 Kinah per node (Inven, exitlag, via daevaguides). Global: unknown; one creator quoted 24,000 Kinah without saying for how many nodes. The app gives no price | daevaguides | KR only; Global price not shown |
| "Acquire All Nodes" | Buys the whole board. Only worth it after 45 when you can afford everything; then remove the weakest nodes | daevaguides | single source |
