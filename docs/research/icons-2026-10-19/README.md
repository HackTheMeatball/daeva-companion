## Method

- **Rules:** docs/research/SOURCES.md row `metabot.gg` (Full; not `/api/` or profile pages) and the owner's standing icon OK
  (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-14). Credit is the plain link to each skill page.
- **Terms** (<https://metabot.gg/en/legal/terms-of-service>, read 6:27 AM ET): effective date 04/01/2026, unchanged since the
  Ranger fetch; "personal, non-commercial use" and the "Data Usage & Attribution (AI Agents & Automated Tools)" section
  (credit MetaBot.GG). My script first tried `/en/terms`, a 404; the terms had been read for the same use on 2026-10-18
  (Ranger icons), within the 24 h cache, and were re-read at the right URL after the run. **robots.txt** (6:20 AM ET):
  `Allow: /`, disallows only `/api/`, `/private/` and profile paths; nothing blocks Claude-User or our User-Agent.
- **Requests:** 60 for the icons (terms attempt, robots, 29 skill pages, 29 image files) plus the terms page, User-Agent
  `DaevaCompanionResearch/0.1 (+https://daevacompanion.com; research probe)`, one at a time, at least 6 s apart (under
  12/min), every one 200 except the 404, no challenge. No page or WebP is kept; only the converted PNGs are committed.

Uncertainty: the file ids are what Metabot served on 2026-10-19 (global client build 0.0.4387.0 per the class CSVs). If
Metabot reissues an icon the file URL may stop resolving; the page URL is the stable credit.
