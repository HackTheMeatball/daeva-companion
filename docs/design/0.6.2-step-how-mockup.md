# 0.6.2: Next up step cards say how, and item mentions show their icon

Release Planner (Walter), 2026-10-18 8:45 PM ET, `design: yes` (#397). Isaac (8:44 PM ET): after Lv 10 the Next up card
"Extract gear you've replaced" doesn't say how. His screens: `/mnt/project-files/owner-screens/2026-10-18/extract/`
(the item tooltip's flask button "Extract", the "Item Extraction" confirm). Cause: `stepHTML` (src/cards/nextup.js)
returns the item's short `ns` title when it has one, else only the step's bold phrase, so every milestone step card drops its how-to although the data has it (steps.json
`g10:0`). Design Lead (Jane) mockup; docs and screenshots only.

- Frames: `docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-step-how/*.png` from its `cases.mjs` (`npm run build && node
  tests/support/shots.mjs docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-step-how --after-only`; `MOCK_BEFORE=1` shoots today's page).
  Before and after pairs: `docs/reviews/0.6.2-mockup-step-how-shots/`. h1-h3 are the how line, i1-i4 the item icons.

## 1. The card

- **Title**: the bold phrase, as today.
- **How line** (new): the step's **first sentence after the bold phrase**, under the title, in body text (`--fg`, 14 px),
  wrapping freely. Tips (`[[tip:…]]`) show as plain words here, with no popover inside a card.
- **Meta line**: "Lv 10 milestone · <milestone link>", muted, as today, now under the how line.
- **The rest** of the step text (the second sentence on, and the step's `note`) goes behind the card's existing `i`
  button into `.ns-info`, the pattern the Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold cards already use. No new control.
- A step with nothing after its bold phrase gets no how line and no `i`, so it looks exactly like today.
- The how line applies to the step kinds that share `stepHTML`: step, missed and optional (adv). Later step rows
  (`laterStep`) stay title-only, because they're a list of what's coming, not something to do now.
- The how line is built from the step's `text` (after its bold phrase), not from the card title, because a step with a
  short `ns` title never reaches the bold-phrase path.
- It still looks like a milestone card: same tile, title, one action. The milestone row already shows the full text,
  so the card is the short form of the same thing, not a second copy.

## 2. Basic mode

- **Basic shows the how line.** It's the essential part: what you click. Isaac hit this in Basic.
- Basic also keeps the `i`: the extra notes are one tap away and take no space until opened.
- Phone stack: as today, only the first card shows its extras; later cards stay title-only.

## 3. For the build

- Where a step's first sentence is long or splits badly, the data can carry an explicit `how` field (steps.json) instead
  of the derived sentence. Inferred: most steps split cleanly at the first ". " after the bold phrase. The build should
  list the steps where it doesn't, rather than guess. A `how` field needs a schema entry (data/schema) and a gen check
  like the step's other text fields.
- Data note for Game Data Check (not design): the confirm screen shows "Required Kina (All) 1,000" for the +1 Training
  Dagger (one owner screen only, so single source), and the extract button is a flask icon in the item tooltip's side bar. The guide says "choose Extract", which
  matches the hover label.

## 4. Item icons where an item is named (i1 to i4)

Isaac, 8:55 PM ET (added to #397): "when we mention the wisdom stones, daevinion crystals, or enhancement stones show a
picture of them in the step." The icons are the app's own sourced files (`assets/icons/wisdom-stone.png`,
`daevanion-crystal.png`, `enhance-stone.png`), so no new art question.

- **Inline icon, 1.15 em**, before the item word, 3 px radius. Inside an item link (`a.itm`) it goes before the
  text and the link's external-link mark stays. The number, icon and word **never wrap apart** ("3 [icon] Wisdom
  Stones" stays on one line).
- **Where:** item links on Next up cards (a step card's title keeps the step's item link, i3), the Cube card's counts
  (i1), the Sealed Dungeon card's reward line behind `i` (i2), and the Dungeons card's "2 Crystals · 2 Wisdom Stones
  each" line (i4, not on the Scrapbook: the six-card limit).
- **Not in milestone rows.** A row that names an item already shows that item's tile on its right, so a second icon in
  its text would repeat it.
- **Never on free text.** Only item links and the reward/count lines the code writes. A "Soul Crystal" or "Life
  Crystal" is not a Daevanion Crystal. The plain word "crystals" on Sealed Dungeon and Cube lines is read as Daevanion
  Crystals (inferred from the Daevanion card's use; Game Data Check can confirm).
- Map icons (Sealed Dungeon, Stronghold, Kibelisk, Empyrean Trace, points of interest) are **not** in this mockup. They
  get their own pass as one consistent set once Source Vetting has looked for HQ copies.
