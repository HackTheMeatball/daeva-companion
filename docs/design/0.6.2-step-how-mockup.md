# 0.6.2: Next up step cards say how, not only what

Release Planner (Walter), 2026-10-18 8:45 PM ET, `design: yes` (#397). Isaac (8:44 PM ET): after Lv 10 the Next up card
"Extract gear you've replaced" doesn't say how. His screens: `/mnt/project-files/owner-screens/2026-10-18/extract/`
(the item tooltip's flask button "Extract", the "Item Extraction" confirm). Cause: `stepHTML` (src/cards/nextup.js)
keeps only a step's bold phrase, so every milestone step card drops its how-to although the data has it (steps.json
`g10:0`). Design Lead (Jane) mockup; docs and screenshots only.

- Frames: `docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-step-how/*.png` from its `cases.mjs` (`npm run build && node
  tests/support/shots.mjs docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-step-how --after-only`; `MOCK_BEFORE=1` shoots today's page).
  Before and after pairs: `docs/reviews/0.6.2-mockup-step-how-shots/`.

## 1. The card

- **Title**: the bold phrase, as today.
- **How line** (new): the step's **first sentence after the bold phrase**, under the title, in body text (`--fg`, 14 px),
  wrapping freely. Tips (`[[tip:…]]`) show as plain words here, with no popover inside a card.
- **Meta line**: "Lv 10 milestone · <milestone link>", muted, as today, now under the how line.
- **The rest** of the step text (the second sentence on, and the step's `note`) goes behind the card's existing `i`
  button into `.ns-info`, the pattern the Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold cards already use. No new control.
- A step with nothing after its bold phrase gets no how line and no `i`, so it looks exactly like today.
- The same applies to the step kinds that share `stepHTML`: step, missed, optional (adv) and the Later step rows. Later
  rows (`laterStep`) stay title-only, because they're a list of what's coming, not something to do now.
- It still looks like a milestone card: same tile, title, one action. The milestone row already shows the full text,
  so the card is the short form of the same thing, not a second copy.

## 2. Basic mode

- **Basic shows the how line.** It's the essential part: what you click. Isaac hit this in Basic.
- Basic also keeps the `i`: the extra notes are one tap away and take no space until opened.
- Phone stack: as today, only the first card shows its extras; later cards stay title-only.

## 3. For the build

- Where a step's first sentence is long or splits badly, the data can carry an explicit `how` field (steps.json) instead
  of the derived sentence. Inferred: most steps split cleanly at the first ". " after the bold phrase. The build should
  list the steps where it doesn't, rather than guess.
- Data note for Game Data Check (not design): the confirm screen shows "Required Kina (All) 1,000" for the +1 Training
  Dagger, and the extract button is a flask icon in the item tooltip's side bar. The guide says "choose Extract", which
  matches the hover label.
