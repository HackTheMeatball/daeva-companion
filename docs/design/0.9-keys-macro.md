## 1. The simple "autopilot" layout: the Rotation section, plus one clause

The owner asked for a simple layout (hold left / right click plus Q / E / R / F) alongside the advanced split.

- **It already exists.** The drawer's Rotation section, above the Key setup fold and always open, is that layout: numbered
  in play order (the E buffs with their disputed tag, Q Shadowstrike, the rest of E, then Hold right + left click), then
  "Right click fires" (the stack, in order) and "Left click" (Quick Slice, which refills the MP right click spends).
  The key grid and the macro are the advanced part, in the Key setup fold, which remembers whether it's open. That is the
  one-view rule (DECISIONS "One view", owner 2026-10-20): one page, the simple part first, detail in a fold, no Simple /
  Advanced switch.
- **One clause added** (Saul, blind review 1): the Hold step reads "Hold right + left click · right click alone drains MP (your
  test)", muted after the dot. It carries the warning and the owner's "(your test)" tag that the removed fold line held.
- **Not R or F.** R isn't a key of its own; by default it's left click's second key (fact 11), so "hold left click"
  covers it. F holds no skill (F1–F8 are consumables, fact 11). The owner's rule that the bar matches the game (beta B4)
  decides this.
- **A class with only default keys** (the Gladiator, `12-sheet-gladiator-*`): its Rotation shows "<key> fires" per key,
  as today. It has no macro in the data, so no macro block is drawn.
- **For the owner:** the Scrapbook caption says "You asked for a Simple mode. It's the Rotation section at the top of
  Build, always open; the keys and macro are in the Key setup fold under it (one view)." That lets the owner object
  without a card.

## 2. The macro as steps

The old fold has three how-to lines ("Put the macro on right click", "Make the macro", "Hold right and left click").
Facts 4–7 and 12 say a macro is an ordered list of steps. Each step fires a whole key's stack or a single skill, there
can be up to 20, and each has its own Delay.

- **Heading:** "Macro on right click" (the drawer's `.khead`, like "Right click fires" and "Coming to E"), or on whichever
  key the data puts it (fact 6). With more than one step: "· 3 steps".
- **One numbered row per step, as in the game's Macro window** (the Rotation's `.playsteps` list, key chip and all):
  - A key step: key chip, the slot's game name ("Quick Use 2"), then muted "· the whole T stack, row 0 first". It
    doesn't list the skills: the T column of the grid in the same fold shows them as icons; each name is in the box's tooltip and accessible name.
  - A single-skill step: icon and name, "· one skill". Only where the data has one (fact 5).
  - A step whose data carries a delay other than 10 ms adds "· Delay 200 ms". No divider, nothing drawn for the default.
- **Under the list, a numbered how-to with bold lead-ins** (owner-ok-0.6.2-icons-locks Q5, "a numbered walk-through"; rev 2.2),
  in the order you do it in game:
  1. "**Make the macro:** press K, open Macro (top right), then Add Macro, one step per line above. Each step's Delay
     starts at 10 ms, the game's default; if ready skills get skipped, raise it there." With 2 or more steps it adds "Up
     to 20 steps. Left click can only be the last step." (facts 6, 7, 12, 12a).
  2. "**Put it on right click** (this guide's choice, not a rule): Esc, Key Settings, General, Gameplay, Macro (no key by
     default, single source), press right click and confirm moving it from its current key (only in the current control
     mode, not AION 1 mode)." "Right click" is the macro's key from the data: another key shows there instead. The one place "not a rule" is said; the Game default keys note stays "The macro takes right
     click from Savage Roar (inferred)".
- **Removed:** the old third line ("Hold right and left click together … Right click alone drains MP; holding left click
  too keeps it full (your test)"). Its action is the Rotation's Hold step, which now carries its warning and tag (§1).
  This is a removal the owner sees (triage trigger 4).
- Steps follow the level like the grid. A step whose key holds nothing yet isn't drawn. A rebound key (#388) shows on the
  step's chip, as on the grid's.
- **Order in the fold:** as #656 drew it (grid, left click, Game default keys, Coming to E, then the macro; on desktop the
  macro is the right column). The fine print after it is unchanged.
- **Cost:** reuses `.rmbline`, `.khead`, `.playsteps`, `.fine`; the build (#853) added `.kv-mac`, `ol.macro-steps`, the
  tooltip rules (`tip-l` / `tip-r`) and `button.kv-key` with its caret. Height at Lv 20, one step, as measured at rev 2 on
  the two-line version (not re-measured for the numbered how-to): 198 px against 186 px at 390, 259 against 245 in the
  320 px drawer. Each further step adds one line.

## The key boxes (rev 2.2)

The boxes stay filled with the icon and the name moves to a tooltip, as asked in the owner's Lv 39 play-test note 12
(project chat 2026-10-20 12:18 PM ET, `release/owner-notes-2026-10-20-lv39.txt` line 55 in project files): "The icons
should fill up the entire space of each "key setup" box instead of being small and having the name below it, mousing
over it or tapping it could bring up a tooltip with the name." That replaces `owner-ok-0.6.2-icons-locks` Q3 (name under
the icon); the Release Planner amends the row.
- **Size:** each box is capped at 88 px at every width (was up to 112 px on a phone), so the grid doesn't outweigh the
  Rotation above it (#853 fresh review 10).
- **Tooltip:** opens on hover, focus or tap, toward the grid's middle, at most `min(220px, 60cqw)` wide in 11 px mono,
  so it stays inside the screen at 360 px (shot: `docs/reviews/0.9-p22-shots/keysetup-lv27-360-tooltip.png`) and, by the
  same rule, at 260 px (not shot); long text wraps, never cutting "row N" (#853 review 1).
- **Key chips:** at least 24 px tall at every width (#853 review 12).

## Accessibility

- The steps are an ordered list. A step reads "1. T Quick Use 2 · the whole T stack, row 0 first"; the key chip is
  text. The mock tag is text.
- The fold's summary is the app's existing Key setup control.
- Each filled box is focusable and named "<skill>, <key>, row N"; its tooltip shows on focus as well as hover and tap.
- Each key chip is a button named "<key>: change the key for this stack". Esc cancels listening, and so do focus
  leaving the chip and closing the drawer.

## Not changed

The Rotation section apart from the Hold step's clause (play order, the C21b disputed tag on the E buffs, Right click fires, Left click, the "How it plays"
tip and its Source line). In the fold: the grid (row 0 at the bottom, DECISIONS `owner-ok-0.9-key-grid`), the filled
icons, rebinding and its clash state, Game default keys with "The macro takes right click from Savage Roar (inferred)",
"Coming to E", and the three fine-print lines, including "Row order and the E stack are the guide's inference … Test on a
training dummy". The desktop panel and phone fold are as in `docs/design/0.9-keys/README.md`.
