## 1. The simple "autopilot" layout: the Rotation section, unchanged

The owner asked for a simple layout (hold left / right click plus Q / E / R / F) alongside the advanced split.

- **It already exists.** The drawer's Rotation section, above the Key setup fold and always open, is that layout: numbered
  in play order (the E buffs with their disputed tag, Q Shadowstrike, the rest of E, then Hold right + left click), then
  "Right click fires" (the stack, in order) and "Left click" (Quick Slice, which refills the MP right click spends).
  The key grid and the macro are the advanced part, in the Key setup fold, which remembers whether it's open. That is the
  one-view rule (DECISIONS "One view", owner 2026-10-20): one page, the simple part first, detail in a fold, no Simple /
  Advanced switch.
- **Not R or F.** R isn't a key of its own; by default it's left click's second key (fact 11), so "hold left click"
  covers it. F holds no skill (F1–F8 are consumables, fact 11). The owner's rule that the bar matches the game (beta B4)
  decides this.
- **A class with only default keys** (the Gladiator, `12-sheet-gladiator-*`): its Rotation shows "<key> fires" per key,
  as today. It has no macro in the data, so no macro block is drawn.
- **For the owner:** the Scrapbook caption says "You asked for a Simple mode. It's the Rotation section at the top of
  Build, always open; the keys and macro are in the Key setup fold under it (one view)." That lets the owner object
  without a card.

## 2. The macro as steps

The old fold has three how-to lines ("Put the macro on right click", "Make the macro", "Hold right and left click").
Facts 4–7 and 12 say a macro is an ordered list of steps. Each step fires a whole key's stack or a single skill, there
can be up to 20, and each has its own Delay.

- **Heading:** "Macro on right click" (the drawer's `.khead`, like "Right click fires" and "Coming to E"), or on whichever
  key the data puts it (fact 6). With more than one step: "· 3 steps".
- **One numbered row per step, as in the game's Macro window** (the Rotation's `.playsteps` list, key chip and all):
  - A key step: key chip, the slot's game name ("Quick Use 2"), then muted "· the whole T stack, bottom row first". It
    doesn't list the skills: the T column of the grid in the same fold shows them, with icons and names.
  - A single-skill step: icon and name, "· one skill". Only where the data has one (fact 5).
  - A step whose data carries a delay other than 10 ms adds "· Delay 200 ms". No divider, nothing drawn for the default.
- **Under the list, two fine lines:**
  - "Make it in game: press K, open Macro (top right), then Add Macro, one step per line above. Each step's Delay starts
    at 10 ms, the game's default; if ready skills get skipped, raise it there." With 2 or more steps it adds "Up to 20
    steps. Left click can only be the last step." (facts 6, 7, 12, 12a).
  - Today's "Put the macro on right click" line, kept with its single-source label, without the arrows.
- **Removed:** the old third line ("Hold right and left click together … Right click alone drains MP; holding left click
  too keeps it full (your test)"). The Rotation's "Hold right + left click" step and "Left click: Quick Slice · refills
  the MP right click spends" already say it. This is a removal the owner sees (triage trigger 4).
- Steps follow the level like the grid. A step whose key holds nothing yet isn't drawn. A rebound key (#388) shows on the
  step's chip, as on the grid's.
- **Order in the fold:** as #656 drew it (grid, left click, Game default keys, Coming to E, then the macro; on desktop the
  macro is the right column). The fine print after it is unchanged.
- **Cost:** no new component or CSS (reuses `.rmbline`, `.khead`, `.playsteps`, `.fine`). Height at Lv 20, one step:
  198 px against today's 186 px at 390, 259 against 245 in the 320 px drawer. Each further step adds one line.

## Accessibility

  text. The mock tag is text.
- The fold's summary is the app's existing Key setup control.
