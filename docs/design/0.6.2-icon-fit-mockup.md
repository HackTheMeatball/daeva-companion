# 0.6.2: icons sit tight, with no dead space around the tile

Isaac, 2026-10-18 10:22 PM ET, on the Sealed Dungeon card: "All icons in any card we create should be aligned in a way
that creates the least dead space." His example: the 52 px tile sits vertically centred in its own column beside four
lines of text, so the column above and below the tile is empty, and every line is pushed right by the tile's width.
Design Lead (Jane) mockup; docs and screenshots only.

- Frames: `docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-icon-fit/*.png` from its `cases.mjs` (`npm run build && node
  tests/support/shots.mjs docs/design/mockups/0.6.2-icon-fit --after-only`; `MOCK_BEFORE=1` keeps today's layout with the
  same icons). Before and after pairs: `docs/reviews/0.6.2-mockup-icon-fit-shots/`.

## 1. The rule

- The **tile lines up with the title only** (same row, centred on it). The title is the one thing the icon labels.
- **Every line under the title runs the card's full width**, starting at the tile's left edge: the place line, the
  reward line, the `i` note, a quest's objectives. Nothing is indented by the tile's width.
- The action button keeps its slot: under everything on a phone, right of the title on a wide card.
- Tile size, dark tile vs red tile, and 70 % icon size are unchanged (#391, #404).

## 2. Where

- Every Next up card (story, step, Sealed Dungeon, Stronghold, region, Cube, …): they share `.ns-mhead`, so this is one
  CSS change in `src/styles/cards/nextup.css` (the `.title` block's lines move to a full-width row), not per card.
- Milestone rows and any new card with an icon follow the same rule (CLAUDE.md: Next up cards look like milestone cards).
  The tracker's cards too (docs/team/design.md).
- The small later-card rows on a phone (34 px tile, one line) already have no dead space and stay as they are.
