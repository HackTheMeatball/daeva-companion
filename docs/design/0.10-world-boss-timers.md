# 0.10: world boss timers (Design Lead, Jane)

The owner's 1.0 must-have (2026-10-20 7:02 PM ET). Walter's draft spec (`/mnt/project-files/plan-0.10/world-boss-timers-draft.md`)
and the data from Game Data Check (#652, `docs/research/2026-10-world-boss-timers.md`). This file decides where the timers
sit and how they look. Mockup only: frames in `docs/design/0.10-world-boss-timers/`, from its `cases.mjs`
(`npm run build && node tests/support/shots.mjs docs/design/0.10-world-boss-timers --after-only`), drawn into today's
Lv 45 page. The frames assume a player on US Eastern (UTC-4) and the research's server clock (UTC+9). Reviewed by an
outside critic (`design-review/critic-0.10-world-boss-timers.md`) and Holly (`design-review/review-0.10-world-boss-holly.md`), answers below.

## Where it lives

**One card, "World bosses"**, shown from Lv 45. It's a reference you glance at, like Dungeons & Strongholds, so it sits
with it:
- On desktop it goes in the rail after Journey, above Dungeons & Strongholds (`wb-card-1500`).
- On a phone it goes right after Next up (`wb-card-390`). At Lv 45 it's one of the essentials (one view, 0.9 P21). Below
  Lv 45 the card doesn't render.

**Next up gets the Executors or Nahma, and only once the clock is confirmed.**
- From 30 min before a spawn until its up window ends, that boss is Next up's first card. Before the spawn the card says
  "Executors spawn in 18 min" (`wb-nextup-soon-*`); after it, "Executors are up · about 19 min left"
  (`wb-nextup-up-*`).
- Their up window isn't sourced, so it's a 30 min default labelled inferred, in a data field `upFor`.
- At most one boss card at a time, the earliest first.
- Watcher Kaira never enters Next up. It spawns every 3 h, so it would take over Next up eight times a day.
- Field bosses never enter Next up either. Their timer depends on a kill the player marks.
- Until the owner confirms the server clock (gate `owner-ok-0.10-boss-clock`), no boss enters Next up: a wrong clock
  would send a player at the wrong hour. The card still shows the times, under "check in game".
- While a boss is in Next up, its card row has no countdown. The row holds an "In Next up" button that scrolls there, so
  the countdown is only ever in one place.
- **Skip ▾** (`wb-nextup-skip-*`) offers "Skip this spawn" or "Don't suggest the Executors". The second is a lasting
  per-boss setting, undone from the card. There's no Done: going to a boss isn't a tick.
- After a skip (`wb-skipped-*`), Next up returns to its own list, and the card row shows the countdown again with
  "Skipped in Next up · Undo". After "Don't suggest" (`wb-not-suggested-*`), the row says "Not suggested in Next up" with
  "Suggest again". That's the only place either is undone.
- The Next up frames (`wb-nextup-soon-*`, `-up-*`, `-skip-*`) show the clock as confirmed, so the card has no "check in
  game" there.

## The card (`wb-card-*`, `wb-running-*`, `wb-check-why-*`, `wb-killed-menu-*`)

- **Abyss · Chaotic Lower Reshanta** gets one "check in game" button, in the pill style, on its subheader. It covers only
  the scheduled bosses. Tapping it opens one line inline that explains the clock dispute, so it works on touch.
  - It goes when the owner confirms the clock.
  - "Inferred" stays as the app's existing text tag (0.8 P20).
- **Each row** follows one pattern: the name; a detail line with where and which days; the countdown on the right; the
  local day and time under it.
  - The countdown reads "1 h 18 min" and "1 d 12 h". The day reads "today" or "tomorrow", and a weekday only after that.
  - Each row's accessible name reads in full ("Executors, in 12 h 48 min, tomorrow 8:30 AM"). Countdowns update once a
    minute, with no `aria-live`.
  - **Watcher Kaira**: "Every 3 h · up 30 min". While it's up, the row reads "up · 18 min left · until 9:30 PM" in the
    good colour.
  - **Executors**: "Argo, Kaira, Tamasa · Mon, Thu, Sat". One row, since they share a slot.
  - **Guardian Lord Nahma** is tagged inferred: "Reshanta's Root · Fri, Sun".
  - Under 30 min the countdown takes the accent text colour, so gold means only "check in game". "Up" is said in words
    as well as colour.
  - The Lv 65 monster level is left out: next to "Lv 45" it reads as a player level.
- **Field bosses · Altgard** shows the faction's own zone. Elyos (1.1) shows "Field bosses · Verteron" with the same three
  bosses and nothing else changed, so no Elyos frame is drawn.
  - **Killed ▾** opens in the row (`wb-killed-menu-*`), with:
    - "Just now", or "At" with a time input;
    - "Or the game's Time Left", with hours and minutes as numeric inputs;
    - a hint: Exploration → Field Boss;
    - Save and Cancel.
    - The chevron flips, and the button carries `aria-expanded`.
  - **States:**
    - Blank: Killed ▾.
    - Counting: "in 4 h 12 min · tomorrow ~1:24 AM", with an "Undo kill" button on its own line.
    - **May be up**, once the cooldown has passed: the detail line reads "May be up since ~1:24 AM", with Killed ▾ back.
      Someone may have killed it again, so it never says "up".
    - One more cooldown after that, the row goes back to blank.
- **Targets:** every button and input is at least 44 px tall on a phone and 32 px on desktop. Undo is a real button.
- **Foot:** "In your time zone (Eastern). Field bosses count from the kill you mark." The zone comes from the browser.
- **No new motion.** Countdowns are text, updated once a minute, with no seconds, pulse or ring.
- **Order:** rows keep a fixed order, so the list doesn't jump.

## No duplication

- A countdown shows in one place: the card, or Next up while that boss is there.
- Dungeons & Strongholds and Journey don't list bosses.
- The Sources card lists the boss sources (the generator does this).

## Not in this mock

- The 4 Middle Reshanta Lv 80 bosses are held out (disputed, owner check 2). If confirmed, they're a third group in the
  same card.
- Notifications outside the page (draft no-go).
- **Field boss spots:** #652 gives the zone only (both Verteron and Altgard). The spot and a map pin are asked of Game Data
  Check. If they're sourced, the detail line gets "spot · pin" like Dungeons & Strongholds; if not, the zone stays.
- **Why go:** the rewards line is left out until a source gives the rewards. The endgame guide's research may.

## Save and data, for the build

- The save gets:
  - field-boss kill times (id → ISO);
  - Next up skips, keyed `wb:<id>@<spawn ISO>`;
  - the "don't suggest" set of boss ids.
  - They're all new fields, so there's no key bump (pre-1.0).
- The data gets `upFor` per scheduled boss: 30 min for Watcher Kaira (sourced), and 30 min inferred for the others.
- The card reads the data's `serverTz`. Changing it moves every countdown (draft acceptance).
