### 1.1 First-run welcome and "I'm already Lv N" (P17)

- **Welcome (W1).** One card above Next up on a fresh browser: a title, three short lines (what the guide is, tick a step
  when you finish it, press Level up when you level up), the sentence "Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with or endorsed
  by NCSoft." (`tests/site.js` keeps that phrase), and two full-width buttons: **Start at Lv 1** (primary) and **I'm
  already Lv …** (secondary). Either one dismisses the welcome for good on this browser.
- **Join path (W2).** The second button swaps the card for one screen: a level slider (the existing level control, 1–45),
  one optional pick, "Story quest you're on", pre-filled with the first story quest at that level, and a three-line
  plain list of what the button will do. One button, **Start at Lv N**, and Back.
- **Asked:** the level; the story quest (pre-filled, accept with one tap). Nothing else.
- **Ticked:** the level, and every story quest before the one picked (derived from the story order, not stored one by
  one if `quest` already implies them, as 0.3's completion does).
- **Not ticked:** regional quests, dungeons, skills, Daevanion nodes, stigmas. We can't know them, and guessing would
  hide real work. Next up's catch-up ranking (0.5 P13) lists what the player may have missed, grouped by band (W3).

**What the save gets (no new key version, all inside `companion-save-v5`):**

| Case | Writes | Doesn't write |
|---|---|---|
| Start at Lv 1 | `welcomed: 1`, a top-level per-device field next to `backup` and `mode` (not a character field, not synced) | nothing on the character |
| I'm already Lv N | `welcomed: 1`; on the current character `level: N` and `quest: <picked story quest>` (the existing character fields) | no `checks`, no `rqSnooze`, no skill, board or stigma fields |
| Reload, either case | nothing; `welcomed` is read, the welcome stays gone | |
| Old v5 save with no `welcomed` | treated as "not welcomed" only when the character is still at Lv 1 with no `checks`; otherwise as welcomed, so no existing player gets the card | |

The join screen is the one-time level setter CLAUDE.md "Manual progress" lets the owner approve (P17 No-gos); after it,
level changes only through Level up / − / + / slider again. The same screen serves **New character** (0.5 P18): "I'm
already Lv N" there sets that character's `level` and `quest` the same way.

### 1.2 Locked cards, hidden vs faded (P16, #254 note 1)

Both variants are drawn for Basic and Advanced at 390 px and 1500 px (L1a/b, L2a/b, DL1a/b, DL2a/b).

- **Hidden.** The card isn't in the page until its unlock is met. At 1500 px the grid has fewer tiles; the page
  reflows when a card appears. Nothing to tab to.
- **Faded.** The card is there from Lv 1 as a dashed shell: the unlock line first ("Opens at Lv 4 · [K]", gold, mono,
  the same style as the unlock text elsewhere), then the card's title, then two grey bars standing for content. No real
  content (no skills, no nodes), so nothing spoils and nothing can be ticked. The shell is one block with the accessible
  name "Skills, opens at Lv 4" and no tab stops inside. When it opens, the shell fills in place: nothing moves.
- **Cards and their unlock (data comes from `unlocks.json` and the owner table, §3):** Skills Lv 4, Daevanion after
  "Urgent Pursuit", Stigmas Lv 22 (owner recollection, matches Metabot; no key shown). Dungeons & Strongholds are the
  fourth gated card; its unlock record is P16's to find, and it stays visible until one is verified.
- **Recommendation (Q1):** faded in **Advanced on desktop**, hidden everywhere else. Reasons, from the frames: the desktop
  grid has room, so faded tiles preview what is coming (DL2b) without anything moving later; on a phone the faded
  shells stack below Next up and push the rest of the page down (L2b adds three shells), and in Basic
  (essentials only) a shell is not an essential. One rule the owner can state: "faded only where it is Advanced and there is
  room".

### 1.3 Key hints (P14)

- **On a Next up row (K1, DK1, W3).** One muted line under the row's text and above its action: "Open Skills: press [K]". It shows only on
  rows whose target card is opened by a key the owner's screens show, and only once that card is open (so never a key for
  a locked card).
- **On the card it opens (K1, DK1).** The key chip in the card header, right-aligned (`aria-label="Key K"`), for the
  card's life, plus a "New" tag in gold on the card for the session in which it opened (Q4).
- **Keys used: only K, U, J, P** (§3). The hint text names the screen, never a mouse path.
- Faded shells show the key in the unlock line (K, U), because the line says when it opens and how to open it then.
  Stigmas show no key: the owner hasn't given one (owner input 3).

### 1.4 One term explanation (P18)

Drawn in T1 (390) and DT1 (1500) with a placeholder term and sentence.

- **How it opens.** The term's **first use on a card** is marked with a dotted underline and a small "?". Tap, click, Enter
  or Space opens the explanation; on desktop hover and focus open it too. Esc or tapping again closes it; one is open at
  a time. It is a disclosure: a `role="button"` with `aria-expanded`, the note linked with `aria-describedby`, not a
  hover-only tooltip.
- **Where it sits.** Directly under the line it explains, inside the card, in the card's inline flow (it pushes the
  content down by its own height, no overlay, nothing clipped on 390 px), using the existing popover look
  (`--panel-2`, `--shadow-popover`).
- **Length cap.** One sentence, at most **140 characters** (P18), our own words, two sources or the owner's screens.
- **Not marked:** later uses on the same card, the Next up rows, the Journey. No term repeats its explanation (no
  redundancy), and at most 12 terms in P18.
