Macro on right click
  1. TQuick Use 2 · the whole T stack, bottom row first

Make it in game: press K, open Macro (top right), then Add Macro, one step per line above. Each step's Delay starts at 10 ms, the game's default; if ready skills get skipped, raise it there.

Put it on right click: press Esc, open Key Settings, General, Gameplay, Macro (no key by default, single source), press right click and confirm moving it from its current key (only in the current control mode, not AION 1 mode).
